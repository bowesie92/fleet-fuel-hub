# Data licences and attribution

This repository and its site are built from public data. Some of it is published here under share-alike licences, which travel with the data. This is a good-faith summary of the providers' terms (checked Nov 17, 2026), not legal advice.

## OpenStreetMap (ODbL 1.0)

Station locations, and the coordinates used as stand-in customer stops, come from OpenStreetMap through the Overpass API. They form a derivative database and are available under the [Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/).

- Files: `data/cache/osm/`, `data/raw/stations.geojson`, `data/raw/customer_points.csv`, and the station fields in `site/data/stations.geojson`.
- Attribution: Data from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).

## openrouteservice results (CC BY-SA 4.0)

Truck routes, route geometry and detour miles and minutes come from the openrouteservice API run by HeiGIT. Its terms state that results are licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). They are cached and republished here under that licence.

- Files: `data/cache/ors/`, and the routing-derived fields in `site/data/routes.geojson`, `site/data/detours.json` and `site/data/detour_paths.geojson`.
- Attribution: © openrouteservice by HeiGIT | Data from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).

## U.S. Energy Information Administration

Weekly retail diesel prices (`data/cache/eia/`, `data/raw/eia_diesel.csv`) come from the EIA Open Data API. EIA does not review or endorse this project. Every station and yard price in this project is synthetic, built from the EIA weekly average as a benchmark (see CLAUDE.md rule 11 for how the site credits EIA).

## Map tiles

The site's base map uses [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org) vector tiles (© OpenMapTiles, data from OpenStreetMap), credited in the corner of every map. Tiles are loaded live and are never cached or committed here; OpenFreeMap's terms do not allow collecting its data in automated ways.

## Everything else

The fleet, its fuel card, telematics and route plan records, and every price shown are **synthetic**, generated by `src/fleetfuel/synth/` from `config/assumptions.yaml`. The MIT License covers the code only; the data files listed above keep their own licences, and the ODbL and CC BY-SA data stay in separate files.
